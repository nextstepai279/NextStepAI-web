Privacy policy

What we collect and why.

Last updated October 13, 2026. NextStepAI is a small AI consulting practice run by Balin and Sam. This page explains, in plain language, what information this website collects and what happens to it.

What we collect

What you tell us in the intake. Your name, role, email, company, website, and your answers about how your business runs. If you use the microphone, your browser turns speech into text before anything is sent; we never receive audio.

How you found us. If you arrive from an ad or a link, we save the campaign details in that link, the page that sent you, and the page you landed on. This lets us see which ads and channels actually help people.

Basic site usage. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and page views. It does not use cookies and does not track you across other websites.

Abuse protection. To stop automated misuse of the intake, we keep a one-way scrambled version of your IP address for up to two days. It cannot be turned back into your IP address.

Booking. If you book a call, Calendly collects your name, email, and chosen time under its own privacy policy.

How we use it

To prepare for and follow up on your strategy call, to reply when you contact us, to understand which of our marketing works, and to keep the site running and protected. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with anyone for their own marketing.

Who processes it for us

A few service providers handle data on our behalf, only to run this site:

  • Cloudflare hosts the site, stores intake conversations, and provides visit counts.
  • Anthropic provides the AI model that reads your intake answers and chooses the next question. Under its commercial terms, Anthropic does not use this data to train its models.
  • Web3Forms delivers your intake answers to our inbox by email.
  • Calendly handles call scheduling.
  • Google Fonts serves the fonts on these pages, which means Google receives your IP address when the page loads.

If we run advertising, platforms such as Google or Meta may receive a notice that someone who clicked an ad later completed the intake or booked a call, so we can measure which ads work. We will list any such platform here when we add it.

How long we keep it

Intake conversations are kept for as long as they are useful for working with you, and deleted on request. Abuse-protection data expires automatically after two days.

Your choices

You can ask us what we have about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, at any time, by emailing hello@nextstepsai.com. You do not have to use the intake; you can email us or book a call directly instead.

Children

This site is for businesses and is not directed at anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
